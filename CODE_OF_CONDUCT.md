# Code of conduct

([Version française](CODE_OF_CONDUCT_fr.md))

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), in short form.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project (issues, discussions, pull requests, translations) to be a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to programming.
- Respect other points of view; criticise the code or the idea, never the person.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sexualised language or images.
- Harassment, public or private.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, issues, pull requests and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban a person temporarily or permanently. This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile [morfredus](https://github.com/morfredus), or use the private reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if it is sensitive. Reports are treated privately and fairly.
